Sign Up

Join CASE

Welcome to our new members. Signing up is free and informal; no obligations whatsoever. You’ll receive community news, invitations to events, and the chance to share your own research within the network.

What you get

Why sign up?

CASE keeps things simple and informal. Signing up means staying connected to a global community working on autonomous discovery.

  • Regular news and updates from the community
  • Invitations to CASE events and seminars
  • A way to promote your own research to the network
