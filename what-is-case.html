What is CASE

What is CASE?

The Community for Autonomous Scientific Experimentation (CASE) connects researchers working on autonomous discovery, self-driving laboratories, and AI-guided experimentation across national labs and universities worldwide. It was founded in April 2021 from a CAMERA workshop, with the organizing committee fully formed by January 2022.

History

The Short History of CASE

The Community for Autonomous Scientific Experimentation (CASE), as an idea, was brought forward during the April 2021 Autonomous Discovery workshop organized by CAMERA.

In the following months, a team of organizers got together to discuss what this community would be all about. The organizing committee fully formed in January 2022 and quickly realized that, while many communities around similar topics existed, they were often difficult to navigate and utilize for communication and collaboration.

This motivated the main mission of CASE: Free and informal information exchange. Every member has the ability to engage, share their research, and communicate with other members.

Mission

Values

Values of CASE

01

Free exchange of ideas and knowledge.

02

Easy access to other members' expertise.

03

Support of new collaborations.

04

Fun events to spread knowledge and engage other members.

05

An accepting and supportive environment.

The emphasis is on simplicity and informality. Every member can use this platform to share any information related to the main topic. This includes advertising conferences, events, software, papers, and any other related information.
